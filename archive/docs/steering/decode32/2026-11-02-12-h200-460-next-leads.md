## P0 — D32-MOE-01: source-exact parallel job-cost scan

### Why this is genuinely a lead

- The active `_fork_native_moe` dispatch selects B4 for 17 < M <= 32 (around source lines 16084–16105) and MAIN above it. That is the decoded T32 MoE path; do not confuse it with verify32/T256.
- In the selected source around **14268–14275**, the x-producer warp calls `wgs_ranges` only when `(TIDX & 31) == 0`. Around **14589–14617**, `wgs_prefix` loops across `nj` jobs serially, constructs both `S.wcp` and `S.wcpB`, and performs the four range-boundary queries serially.
- An earlier distinct source body, around **5701–5708** and **6127–6157** in the same release file, has a real all-32-lane `__shfl_up_sync` prefix implementation, with four lanes deriving the boundaries. It has a **different cost formula** that adds per-unit terms. It is an implementation reference, **not a drop-in snippet**: the active cost formula is `(uint32_t)wgs_cost(pb,S.jC[j]) << (9-pb)`.
- The external note reports ~0.9% for a warp-parallel job-cost scan at 32 tokens, but supplies no exact code or receipts. This merely raises the priority of the source-visible serial bottleneck.

## What the ~460 note changes, and what it does not

The useful general shift is **look at startup, per-CTA work assignment, cross-phase handoff and tails when steady-state weight streaming is close to a physical bandwidth ceiling**. It is not a command to copy an absolute 4.2–4.45TB/s “roofline” onto our timed layer, whose bytes, caching and path differ. Its “cold L2/adjusted event” guidance favors our existing matched/duplicate-state and replay gates; do not change service-adjusted timing constants to imitate the external harness. Its failed four-CTA clusters, speculative weight prefetch and four-stage/deeper rings are not fresh D32 P0 work.

The current public607 retains required SC6 packing, shape-specialized Tiny/Tiny2/MID/B4/MAIN code, staged weights and data-flag phase-B readiness. `GOPT-PIPE-01` already owns a current skinny NS3/NS4 check for T1/T8/T16 and **does not modify B4**. CUDA Optimizer owns separate scale/group work and has already measured scale-only without a repeatable win. Leave both current jobs and their artifacts alone.
