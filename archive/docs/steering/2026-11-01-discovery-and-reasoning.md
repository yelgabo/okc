There is a second independent failure mode. In mode 79 check 2a4fb39964aa448c, the owned implementation lost while the donor arm won on the same layer-style routing. The experiment justified closing that implementation. It left a positive control whose advantage we could not reproduce. Queueing unrelated small changes leaves that discrepancy open. A stronger research question would have been which consumers need which producer results before proceeding. Applied to the final aggregate, it identifies a token's contributing experts. Feasibility still requires proving visibility, progress and safe reuse. Neither reasoning exercise predicts the aggregate gain.

Other blind spots include strong individual shapes hidden by a mediocre aggregate, public code under a collaborator or fork rather than the leaderboard handle, shape-dispatch or preparation effects that disappear when only a kernel body is ported, and fast timings that change precision or depend on invalid synchronization. Private implementations remain unobservable. Each can change the choice of experiment.

The coordinator now requires an owner-independent source survey and an explanation of the strongest unresolved comparator before renewing an experiment family. Record dated queries, coverage limits, pins and receipts. Compare a complete conforming pipeline on matched workloads before assigning gains to parts. Stop local tuning where its measured ceiling cannot close the targeted gap. Validate the process prospectively on newly published sources and record misses and discovery delay. Today's retrospective retrieval cannot certify future performance. This task used no GPU checks or public submissions.
