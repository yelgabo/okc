How to interpret the squares: The same orange squares persist through the animation to show the movement of feature positions. A matrix multiplication creates new values by combining many inputs; it is not a literal one-to-one transfer of feature values. The small numerical examples are illustrative, not sampled model tensors. Scroll horizontally over the diagram on a narrow screen.

What happens after MoE? Is that a new token?

No. Finishing this MoE block finishes the feed-forward portion of one transformer layer. The current token's hidden representation continues through the remaining layers. Only after the model's final layer and output head do you get logits for selecting a next token.
