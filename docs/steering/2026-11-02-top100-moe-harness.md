## Frozen paired performance decision

A256MiB flush precedes each event interval; only the complete component graph lies between start/end events. Correctness checks, poisoning, guard checks and host counters are outside the interval. Timings are raw event intervals, without borrowed judge-overhead corrections or instrumentation kernels inside the graph. Actual H200 cold-cache behavior and event accuracy remain unmeasured.

For every cross `(0,2),(0,3),(1,2),(1,3)`, each swapped pair contributes the mean of its two log time ratios; the per-load ratio is the exponentiated median of four such paired log values. Thus decisions use paired ratios, **not ratios of separately aggregated median times**. Per-placement medians are retained descriptively. Duplicate-control and duplicate-candidate nulls are the mean absolute swapped-pair log ratios for `(0,1)` and `(2,3)` separately.

For **each bank and each distribution separately**, all six loads have equal weight: every cross's geometric gain must be>=1.01, and its mean log gain must exceed twice the larger of the two equal-six duplicate log nulls. This is stronger than pooling normal/skewed or banks. Any>=1% candidate-time regression on any affected timed load (M32/128/256), any bank/distribution/cross, blocks advancement. M1/8/16 unchanged-path drift is explicitly returned by the reader, never hidden.
