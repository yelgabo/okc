## Experiment order and stopping rules

1. **Whole-layer native experts.** G1a takes 24.9 µs in the expert kernel at sliding decode 1 and 221.4 µs at full verify 32. First establish native backend selection, legal setup time, all 13 correctness cases and all 12 medians for G1b2. Reconstruct the official six-load score from the receipt before considering a release. Subsequent claimed wins need matched fresh inputs, retained output/ordering obligations and a gain over twice the duplicate-control log variability on independent seeds.
2. **Expert completion and packing.** G0 bundles MFIN and SC6. The exact standalone public MoE check b65c4c878014416c reached 2.395703×, still below 2.453388× for rank 50 in the snapshot. Keep component and layer scores distinct. Next isolate lossless SC6 and a generation-safe per-token finalizer with matched routing, including empty and heavily skewed expert sets. Compare the full scored loads and use the current G path as control. A bounded spin that proceeds without observing completion is a correctness failure. [Standalone measurements](2026-11-01-moe-architecture-comparison.md). [Earlier mode 79 and proposed controls](2026-11-01-keell-backlog.md).
3. **Low-token boundaries.** The fused pooler/router is already active at short decode. In the earlier owned T1 trace top-k completed 11.8/11.2 µs before normalization in two examples. The fused G1a kernel's 18.6 µs is total duration, not avoidable traffic. Measure producer completion, consumer start, bytes and the exposed reduction in a matched full graph before implementing another fusion. At the final boundary investigate the existing 1024-channel slice and its BF16 norm reduction. Stop if the measured exposed time cannot cover the target's weighted score budget. [Interface and timeline audit](2026-11-01-fusion-opportunity-audit.md). [Beyond-G scenario and required budget](2026-11-01-beyond-family-g.md).
4. **Standalone projection.** Compare owned dispatch against the four largest observed absolute comparator leads at pooler128, QKV128, router256 and unpooler128. The candidate must maintain the FP32 reduction contract, pass held-out tests and improve the complete 30-shape weighted score beyond duplicate noise. The external 1.573016× comparator does not itself reach the observed 1.579078× rank 50. Repeating the failed QKV epilogue or transpose alone does not address those four shapes. [Projection medians](evidence/2026-11-01-top50/projection-external-shapes.md).
5. **Standalone attention.** In f74cbe27819147c1 the owned two-cluster merge took 32.22/32.03 µs at batch-1 sliding verify versus 11.17 µs controls. Both builds used 111 registers, zero local bytes and 90704 dynamic shared bytes, with two blocks per SM and a queried capacity of 14 clusters. The corrected restricted six-load ratio was 0.846483. The global-finalize-preserving variant is closed. The distinct one-head, cluster-local finalizer warrants another check only after proving complete output ownership and a score opportunity worth the duplicate noise.
