## Exact eligibility eliminates the tempting fast-path stories

The A16 non-dense overlap policy (sm90_policies476–575) can propose WGMMA, block-K64, warp-K64,4stages, warp specialization/TMA, split-issue-wait, no stream-K. Shapes N8192/4096,K4096 pass its divisibility/long-K checks, but resource fitting, estimated grid and policy selection still decide whether it is returned. This is a source-feasible family, not an asserted selected BM/BN or a fixed tuning candidate. BF16 gives part-K16 and **4warp fragments** at warp-K64. The late-scale safeguard requires exactly1fragment: it is **false in that A16 overlap configuration**, not a new BF16/NVFP4 acceleration. Other legal configs cannot be inferred from that seed.

- Native low-bit hardware conversion is disabled by config.py196–199 for SM9x; BF16 native-dequant low-bit predicates additionally require newer architecture-family support. No Hopper direct FP4→BF16 instruction-path win comes from this branch.
- ldmatrix-dequant eligibility is integer INT8/INT4 or INT4/INT2, not BF16/E2M1. Packed-K's explicit API assertion requires8-bit activations. Do not smuggle either into BF16 Muse.
- Fused E8M0/MXFP4 decode is not E4M3/group16 NVFP4. For BF16/E2M1/E4M3/group16, software decode and BF16 group-scale multiplication remain necessary. mainloop_arith156–239 applies those scales on the weight operand, not a substitute post-dot single group scale.

The only surviving split-issue mechanism hides shared-load latency that native already attempts to cover with its double buffer and outstanding group. Humming's per-fragment full drain is not an identified improvement over that. Wider fragments, extra stages, residency or better cache behavior could still win, but **none follows as a substantial positive mechanism from the traced source**, and changing those together would be an unpriced configuration experiment rather than this proposed overlap attribution.
