November 1. Evidence through 8e3b681, standalone comparator b65c4c878014416c, G1b2 layer 736a5cbc977f4ad4, and controlled standalone SC6 comparison 05e51ddeb7014dd9. The latter passed all five official tests. Changing only lossless scale storage failed its registered six-load gate. The next test examines when output finalizers can run with real standalone routing.

The check keeps the repaired byte/SC6 study's native expert math, resident cooperative grid, five BF16 boundaries and disabled MFIN. Both timing arms use SC6, the same immutable weight layout, the same 256-expert source weights, actual global IDs and private scratch. The judged path uses the uninstrumented kernel. Two control graphs and a stamped graph rotate through 33 cold replays per input. They share fresh input buffers but maintain separate state and output. The test validates initial outputs, all three outputs after timing, fresh replays and distinct all-nonlocal routes against the baseline or exact zero. This is off-board component research only.

Each writer warp records its last entry into a real phase-B epilogue for each local expert. The sample precedes a store and is deliberately optimistic about when the finalizer could see completed writes. It cannot prove publication or replace a fence. The existing CTA join before the grid barrier supplies the owner endpoint. Per-token start and end samples bracket the existing full-row finalizer. The offline replay keeps each token on its original CTA and preserves token order. A CTA starts the next token only after its own compute, all required expert issue samples and its previous token. The comparison treats new publication and retirement overhead as zero. The result sizes this specific fixed-owner alternative on the instrumented schedule. No new scheduling policy runs in this check.

Gate registered before dispatch. Require six cases at 128/256 tokens on three held-out routing seeds, five separately cleared cold trace samples per case, reference/fresh/empty checks, exact route coverage and reconstitution of the designated trace. Require matching H200 true-specialization local bytes, static/dynamic shared memory, CTA residency and block size, with no spills. Reject quantitative extrapolation if duplicate control latency differs by over 2% in log units or stamped latency differs from the geometric mean control by over 5%. Use the geometric mean of all six workload ratios, holding the four unaffected loads at one. If even the most favorable sample stays below 1% on all seeds, park fixed-owner finalization. If the least favorable sample clears 1% and twice the maximum duplicate-control log variation on every seed, consider a separate implementation with proven publication, generation and reader retirement. Otherwise the result is unresolved. No public promotion follows from this attribution run.
