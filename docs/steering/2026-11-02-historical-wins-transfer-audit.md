## What survived and what is missing

Most reviewed attention wins and several expert-loop wins have counterparts in G1b4. Different flag names hid that continuity. The clearest missing small operator is C2's register-direct O8 projection. The largest missing old scheduling mechanism is dynamically claimed phase B, but G has replaced the old imbalance-producing partition with weighted stage ranges. Neither observation establishes a new gain on G.

The useful comparison is between complete current operations on the same inputs. Restoring a pooler or router in isolation can discard the fused operation that replaced it. Removing a fence or restoring an old arithmetic shortcut can discard a correctness repair. Old component gains cannot be summed or transferred into a new layer score.
